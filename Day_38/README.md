# Day 38 — PostgreSQL: Alias, GROUP BY & HAVING

## What I Learned
- **Alias (`AS`)** — Rename result columns.
- **GROUP BY** — Group rows by a shared value.
- **COUNT(*)** — Count rows in each group.
- **HAVING** — Filter groups based on aggregate results.
- **ORDER BY DESC** — Sort results from highest to lowest.

## Practice
Counted people by country, kept countries with at least two people, and sorted by the number of people in descending order.
